# Non-Play-Store Policy (Current Project Direction)

Created: 2026-03-19
Status: Project direction note

## Summary

The Android companion app is not constrained by Play Store requirements at this stage. Full capability and practical detection are prioritized over store compliance.

## Implications

- Privileged signals may be used when available (e.g., system logs, supplicant reason codes).
- Features blocked by Play Store policies may still be implemented for research-grade and real-world use.
- Play Store compliance can be evaluated later as a separate track.

## Notes

- This policy does not change the security guardrails in docs/SECURITY.md.
- This policy does not permit offensive functionality.
